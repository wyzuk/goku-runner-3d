### 3D Models & Geometry
- **3D Goku Character Model**: 100% Original custom procedural hierarchical geometry, rigged Saiyan bone structure, anime cel-shaded materials, animated spiky hair, orange Gi, blue undershirt, blue wristbands, blue belt, and blue boots created specifically for this project by Wyzuk.
- **Environment & Props (Trees, Cliffs, Ruins, Floating Sky Islands, Kami's Realm, Boulders, Arches)**: Custom procedural 3D models and geometry created by Wyzuk using Three.js procedural meshes, custom anime vertex shading, and optimized instanced rendering.
- **Collectibles (Dragon Balls, Ki Energy Orbs, Flying Nimbus, Senzu Bean, Ki Boost, Magnetic Aura, Zenkai Boost)**: Custom 3D procedural models with real-time internal refraction, rotating star cores, and emissive glow shaders by Wyzuk.

### Audio & Sound FX
- **Procedural Web Audio Engine**: Custom synthesizers generated in real-time via the Web Audio API (footstep thuds, aerodynamic wind whooshes, sliding ground friction, chime resonators for Dragon Balls, ethereal frequency modulation for Ki orbs, dramatic charging blast sounds for Super Saiyan transformation, and multi-track retro-synth arcade background soundtrack) programmed by Wyzuk. No copyrighted or third-party audio files required; zero external bandwidth or licensing dependencies.

### Shaders & Visual Effects
- **Anime Cel Shading & Rim Lighting**: Custom Three.js shader modifications, ramp lighting, cel bands, and rim highlights by Wyzuk.
- **Super Saiyan Aura & Lightning**: Custom procedural dynamic noise shader aura mesh, particle spark generators, and randomized branch lightning ribbons by Wyzuk.
- **Speed Lines & Particle Systems**: Custom dynamic GL point & billboard particles by Wyzuk.
